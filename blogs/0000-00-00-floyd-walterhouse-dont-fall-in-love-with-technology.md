This article profiles Floyd Walterhouse, McKee Foods' longest-serving IT leader with nearly 48 years at the company, tracing his perspective on technology adoption from PeopleSoft through Workday's early days as their seventh customer. He emphasizes that "the person on the other side of the technology is the point," advocating thoughtful AI implementation rather than simply overloading employees with additional tasks.
